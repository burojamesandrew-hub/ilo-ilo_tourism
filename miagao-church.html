Miagao Church

Overview

Miagao Church, also known as the Church of Santo Tomas de Villanueva, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site famous for its unique Baroque architecture and intricate facade blending Spanish and Filipino influences. It stands as a symbol of Iloilo’s rich colonial history.

Top Attractions

Intricate Baroque facade

Massive coral stone structure

Historic bell towers

Church interiors and altar

Things To Do

Explore the architecture

Attend a mass

Take historical photos

Learn local history

Travel Information

  • Best Time: Year-round
  • Location: Miagao, Iloilo
  • Nearest Transport: Miagao town terminal

Located in Miagao, about 40 km from Iloilo City. Accessible via bus or jeepney from the city.
