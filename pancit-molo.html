Pancit Molo

Experience the rich culture, historic landmarks, beautiful islands, and vibrant lifestyle of Iloilo.

Start Exploring

Overview

Pancit Molo is a Filipino dumpling soup originating from Molo district, Iloilo. It consists of pork-filled wontons in savory broth and is considered comfort food.

Top Attractions

Delicate Dumpling Soup

Clear, Flavorful Broth

Garlic and Onion Aroma

Comforting and Light Texture

Top Ingredients

Pork-filled wontons
Chicken or pork broth
Garlic and onions
Green onions

Travel Information

  • Best Time: Anytime, great for lunch or merienda
  • Location: Molo, Iloilo City
  • Nearest Transport: Jeepney routes to Molo
  • Found mostly in Molo district, Iloilo City. Accessible via jeepneys or tricycles.

Travel Tips

Try it with chili vinegar for authentic flavor

Visit small eateries for a traditional experience

Take home some frozen dumplings if available

Suggested Itinerary

Enjoy Pancit Molo for lunch, then visit Molo Church and nearby heritage sites.
