Chicken Inasal

A famous Iloilo grilled chicken dish with rich marinade and smoky flavor.

Start Exploring

Overview

Chicken Inasal is a popular grilled chicken dish from Iloilo, marinated in calamansi, vinegar, garlic, and annatto oil, then grilled over hot coals.

Highlights

Distinctive Marinade

Smoky Flavor

Juicy Meat

Signature Sides

Top Ingredients

Chicken
Calamansi
Garlic & Vinegar
Annatto Oil

Travel Information

  • Best Time: Lunch or dinner
  • Location: Iloilo City
  • Nearest Transport: Jeepneys
  • Available across the city in restaurants and grills.

Tips

Try with vinegar sauce
Eat while hot
Look for charcoal grills

Suggested Experience

Enjoy Chicken Inasal at a local restaurant, then explore nearby areas like the Iloilo River Esplanade.
