Islas De Gigantes

Experience the rich culture, historic landmarks, beautiful islands, and vibrant lifestyle of Iloilo.

Start Exploring

Overview

Islas de Gigantes is one of the most breathtaking island groups in Iloilo, known for its powdery white sand beaches, dramatic limestone cliffs, and crystal-clear turquoise waters. It is a top destination for island hopping, offering a perfect blend of adventure and relaxation.

Top Attractions

Cabugao Gamay Island viewpoint

Antonia Beach

Tangke Saltwater Lagoon

Gigantes Norte Lighthouse

Things To Do

Island hopping
Swimming
Explore caves
Seafood dining

Travel Information

  • Best Time: November to May
  • Location: Carles, Iloilo
  • Nearest Transport: Bancal Port
  • Located in Carles, Iloilo. Travelers can take a bus or van from Iloilo City to Carles, then ride a boat.

Travel Tips

Bring cash as ATMs are limited

Use sunscreen and stay hydrated

Book island tours in advance

Suggested Itinerary

Morning

Boat ride and Cabugao Gamay

Afternoon

Lunch at Antonia Beach

Evening

Swim at Tangke Lagoon
