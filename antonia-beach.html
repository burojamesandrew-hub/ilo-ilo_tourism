Antonia Beach

Start Exploring

Overview

Antonia Beach is a stunning white sand beach located in the Gigantes Islands. Known for its fine sand and calm waters, it is a perfect stop for relaxation during island hopping tours.

Top Attractions

Powdery white sand beach

Clear shallow waters

Peaceful environment

Scenic coastal views

Things To Do

Swim in calm waters
Relax on the beach
Take photos
Enjoy picnic meals

Travel Information

  • Best Time: Summer months
  • Location: Gigantes Islands
  • Nearest Transport: Bancal Port
  • Located in Islas de Gigantes and accessible via boat from Bancal Port in Carles.

Suggested Itinerary

Arrive via island hopping tour

Afternoon

Relax, swim, and enjoy lunch by the beach
