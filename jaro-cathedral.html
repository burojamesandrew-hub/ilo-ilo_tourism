Jaro Cathedral

The only cathedral in the Philippines with a separate belfry — patroness of all dioceses in Western Visayas.

Start Exploring

Overview

Jaro Cathedral, officially known as the National Shrine of Our Lady of the Candles, is one of the most important religious landmarks in Iloilo. It is famous for its separate bell tower and the miraculous Marian image that draws devotees from across the Philippines.
