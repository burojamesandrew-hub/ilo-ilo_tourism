Sicogon Island

A tropical paradise with pristine white beaches, crystal blue waters, and a laid-back island atmosphere in Carles.

Start Exploring

Overview

Sicogon Island is a serene tropical destination known for its pristine beaches, lush landscapes, and peaceful environment. It offers a relaxing escape from busy city life while showcasing the natural beauty of Iloilo.

Top Attractions

Sicogon Beach

Mt. Opao hiking trail

White sand shoreline

Resort areas

Things To Do

Relax by the beach
Hike scenic trails
Swim and snorkel
Watch the sunset

Travel Information

  • Best Time: December to May
  • Location: Carles, Iloilo
  • Nearest Transport: Bancal Port
  • Accessible via Carles port. From Iloilo City, travel by land to Carles, then take a boat to Sicogon Island.

Travel Tips

Book accommodations early

Bring essentials for island stay

Respect local communities
