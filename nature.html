Top Destinations

Bucari Pine Forest

A cool mountain retreat known for its towering pine trees, misty trails, and peaceful natural scenery—perfect for hiking and nature walks.

Bulabog Puti-an National Park

A protected area featuring lush forests, waterfalls, and diverse wildlife—ideal for eco-adventures, trekking, and nature photography.

Garinfarm

A unique agri-tourism and pilgrimage destination where visitors can explore farm life, scenic landscapes, and a towering cross overlooking the countryside.

Tangke Saltwater Lagoon

A hidden natural pool surrounded by limestone cliffs, perfect for swimming, relaxing, and taking stunning photos.
