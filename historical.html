Explore Iloilo’s Rich Heritage

Journey through Iloilo’s storied past, where grand churches, historic streets, and centuries-old landmarks tell the tales of a vibrant culture. From architectural marvels to bustling heritage districts, immerse yourself in the history that shaped the heart of the Visayas.

Top Destinations

Molo Church

A historic church known for its Gothic design and unique all-female saints, making it one of Iloilo’s iconic landmarks.

Miagao Church

A UNESCO World Heritage Site known for its intricate Baroque facade and rich colonial history.

Jaro Cathedral

A prominent religious landmark known for its separate bell tower and the revered Our Lady of the Candles shrine.

Calle Real

A historic street lined with heritage buildings, showcasing Iloilo’s rich cultural and commercial past.
